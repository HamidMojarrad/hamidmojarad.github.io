% Computational Linguist specializing in bias mitigation for speech and language technologies, focusing on underrepresented dialects, particularly African American English (AAE). My research combines sociolinguistics and machine learning to improve the fairness and reliability of ASR and NLP systems. I work with transformer-based models (e.g., wav2vec 2.0, Whisper, BERT) for morphosyntactic and phonological feature detection in AAE, and optimize forced alignment tools such as Montreal Forced Aligner and Aeneas to enhance audio-text alignment robustness for sociolinguistic research.

% I primarily use Python with libraries like PyTorch and Hugging Face Transformers, along with NLP tools for data analysis and model development. I collaborate with linguists and computer scientists to develop more inclusive speech technology solutions. My earlier background in software engineering helps me apply robust development practices and interdisciplinary problem-solving in computational linguistics. \\
